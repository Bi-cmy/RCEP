# Decision: VIABLE EXPLORATORY, with trend-continuation risk

The design passes its frozen support-informed gates. The 2020 interaction
relative to 2021 is 0.026777 (p=0.2596), below the 0.05 economic threshold.
The static post interaction is -0.054174 per additional pre-2020 architecture
(firm-clustered SE 0.023079, p=0.0190). Its 999-draw stratified randomization
p-value is 0.001. The two-plus-architecture and active-country specifications
have the same sign, and none of 18 leave-one-industry-out estimates reverses
sign.

This is not a fully outcome-blind confirmatory result: the Experiment 07
support counts were known before this PAP. More importantly, only one clean
pre-policy interaction is available. Its sign implies a relative decline from
2020 to 2021 for more-exposed firms, so the post-policy negative pattern may be
a continuation of a differential trend rather than an RCEP break. Experiment
10 was frozen separately to test that possibility. The detrended post break is
-0.021191 (p=0.4694; stratified-randomization p=0.247), so the finding does not
survive the trend-continuation sensitivity. It may be retained as a disclosed
exploratory consolidation pattern, but not as a positive causal headline.
