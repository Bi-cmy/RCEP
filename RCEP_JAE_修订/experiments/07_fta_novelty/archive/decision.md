# Decision: FAILED at balanced-panel support gate

Closed: 2026-07-23

## Exposure support

The non-overlapping design successfully constructs the pre-2020 institutional
readiness measure. Among eligible balanced firms with pre-policy industry and
size data, 261 represent at least one prior institutional architecture and 52
represent at least two. Both frozen exposure thresholds (250 and 40) pass.
The sample spans 18 industries and 50 industry-size permutation strata; only
two exposed firms are in singleton strata. At least 213 firms have a positive
primary outcome in every outcome year.

## Failed condition

The frozen PAP required at least 3,000 firms with all five outcome years and
valid frozen industry and size characteristics. The constructed sample has
2,556. The panel-support gate therefore fails even though outcome and exposure
variation are adequate.

## Decision

No pretrend, post-RCEP interaction, stratified permutation, robustness,
mechanism, or heterogeneity coefficient was estimated. The 3,000-firm threshold
was not lowered after observing 2,556, and the balanced-panel rule was not
replaced by an unbalanced specification.

Conceptually this remains stronger than an average-relationship-age mechanism:
it maps directly to RCEP's unification of previously separate rules-of-origin
architectures and uses non-overlapping exposure and outcome windows. It would
require a new PAP with an independently justified sampling rule or additional
data, not a retroactive amendment to this experiment.
