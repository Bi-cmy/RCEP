# Experiment 07: cross-agreement institutional readiness

## Question

Did firms whose pre-2020 supplier networks crossed more of the previously
separate China-Asia trade-agreement architectures expand RCEP supplier links
more after the common RCEP framework entered into force?

## Design distinction

Exposure is measured only in 2017-2019. Outcomes begin in 2020, so the clean
pre-policy diagnostic years do not overlap the exposure window. The exposure
is a proxy for potential gains from institutional unification and cumulation,
not observed use of an RCEP certificate.

## Final status

Failed at the frozen balanced-panel support gate. Exposure variation passed,
but only 2,556 firms had the required five outcome years and pre-policy
characteristics, versus the pre-registered minimum of 3,000. No coefficient
was estimated. See `archive/decision.md`.
