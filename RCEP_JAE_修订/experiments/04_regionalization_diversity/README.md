# Experiment 04: within-firm Asian supplier-link reallocation

## Question

After RCEP entry, did covered Chinese listed firms reallocate active supplier
relationships toward RCEP countries relative to Asian non-RCEP countries?

The design uses a balanced firm-country-year candidate panel and absorbs every
firm-year shock. It studies the location of active links within the firm's
Asian network, not the firm's total number of links.

## Current phase

The PAP is frozen. No support statistic, pre-trend, or policy coefficient has
been inspected.
