# Decision: FAILED at data-support gate

Closed: 2026-07-23

The fixed 2019 cohort contains 636 supplier relationships: 468 with RCEP
countries and 168 with Asian non-RCEP controls. Ten RCEP countries and six
control countries are represented, for 16 partner-country clusters. The panel
is balanced for 2020-2024. These support conditions passed.

The preferred relationship and firm-year fixed-effect model requires within-
firm treatment-group overlap. Only 48 listed firms had both an RCEP and an
Asian-control supplier relationship in the frozen cohort. The PAP required at
least 50, so the within-firm support gate failed.

No pretrend, static policy coefficient, bootstrap, label randomization,
recovery decomposition, robustness check, mechanism, or heterogeneity result
was estimated. The threshold was not lowered after observing that it missed by
two firms, and the preferred firm-year fixed effects were not replaced by a
weaker common-year specification.
