# Experiment 06: pre-pandemic supplier-link resilience

## Question

Did RCEP entry improve the continuity or reactivation of supplier relationships
that were already active before the COVID-19 disruption?

## Design

The cohort is frozen at year-end 2019. The preferred model uses relationship
and listed-firm-by-year fixed effects, country-specific RCEP timing, and
partner-country clustered inference. No relationship is selected according to
whether it later disappears or reappears.

## Final status

Failed at the frozen within-firm support gate. Only 48 firms held both RCEP and
Asian-control cohort relationships, while the PAP required 50. No coefficient
was estimated. See `archive/decision.md`.
